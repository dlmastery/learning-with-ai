## Definition of done per section

- July 2026 cutoff and latest available primary sources.
- Clear separation of `MEASURED-RCT`, `MEASURED-BENCH`, `OBSERVED`, `VENDOR`,
  and `INFERENCE`.
- Direct consequence for the universal mentor architecture.
- Rural, low-connectivity, multilingual, and accessibility implications.
- At least one exact visual with useful alt text.
- Cross-links to related sections and the reference architecture.
- Link and Markdown validation before commit.
