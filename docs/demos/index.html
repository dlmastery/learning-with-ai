Demo Gallery

Every technique, running

A survey that only describes techniques is a reading list. Each demo here runs in your browser — no server, no key, no sign-in — and states plainly what it proves and what it merely illustrates. Cards marked Building are not yet written and are deliberately not linked, rather than shipped as a dead link.

Computed live Scripted illustration Shows a null result Building — not yet written

Two kinds of demo here, and the difference matters more than the polish. Computed demos actually perform the operation — the grounding ladder really runs its checks and really times them; the pāṭha protocol really permutes and really detects. Scripted demos replay a realistic transcript because the underlying behaviour needs a model we can't ship in a static page. Every scripted demo is labelled as such, on the page, above the fold. None of them are dressed up as measurements.

The honest caveat that applies to all of them. A demo shows that a mechanism is buildable and coherent. It is not evidence that it teaches. The evidence lives in the survey, the effect sizes are on the dashboard, and several of these techniques have never been tested against a proper control — which is stated on the pages where it's true.

What none of these are

They are not products, not a curriculum, and not evidence of learning gain. They exist so that a reader can disagree with a specific thing rather than with a description of a thing. Every demo links back to the research report it came from, and every report is in research/raw/ with its sources.
