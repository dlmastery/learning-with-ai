The Archivist

A learner model that is actually persistent, entirely visible as JSON, and genuinely destroyable — with the read-back that proves the destruction happened. Everything a tutor believes about you should be a thing you can read and contradict.

Computed live Writes to your browser Never measured vs stateless
What this is

A persistent learner model living in your browser's own storage, shown as raw JSON. Teach it something, watch its per-concept estimates move, then correct it or destroy it.

Why it matters

Persistent state is the single largest unmeasured engineering commitment in this field — no study compares a stateful tutor against a stateless one. The most-adopted AI tutor ever built (29,606 stars) opens its README with DISCONTINUED after failing twice to persist a learner model.

How to use it

Answer a few items and watch the estimates update. Then correct an entry the model got wrong — a learner and a parent must be able to. Finally press delete and re-read the record: under IDEA §300.624 that deletion has to be real, not a flag.

COMPUTED. This page writes a real record to localStorage on your device, under the key —. Nothing is sent anywhere — there is no server on the other end of this page. The knowledge estimate is Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (Corbett & Anderson 1995), computed here, with its four parameters exposed and editable. The delete button really deletes, and the receipt is a genuine read-back, not a message.
localStorage is unavailable in this context. The page has fallen back to memory only, so the model will not survive a reload. Everything else works. This is disclosed rather than hidden, because a tutor that silently loses your record is worse than one that never had it.

4 · Deletion, and the part deletion cannot reach

Deletion receipt
Produced by reading the key back out of storage after the removal. If the key still held anything, this box would say so.
No deletion performed in this session.

34 CFR § 300.624 — Destruction of information — is not advisory. When personally identifiable information is no longer needed to provide educational services, the agency must inform parents, and, verbatim: “The information must be destroyed at the request of the parents.” Only a minimal permanent record — name, address, phone, grades, attendance, classes attended, grade level and year completed — may be kept without time limit.

The architectural consequence, which is the actual finding. You can delete the row. You cannot delete the influence. If a child's interaction history has been folded into model weights, into a shared embedding index, or into an aggregate learner-model prior, the destruction request cannot be honoured — not as a policy failure but as a mathematical one. Any product touching special-education populations therefore has to store per-learner state in a form that is genuinely deletable, and must never use it for cross-learner training without irreversible, pre-storage de-identification. § 300.624 makes that a precondition, not a feature. It is also why this demo keeps everything in one JSON blob under one key: deletability is a schema decision, and it has to be made before the first write.
And the flip side, stated with equal force. Writing suspected_dyslexia: 0.72 to a durable per-child record creates a health-data dossier under GDPR Art. 9(1), a retained record under COPPA § 312.10, and an IDEA-destroyable record under § 300.624 — with none of the clinical process, none of the appeal rights, and none of the accuracy guarantees of an actual diagnosis. Persistence is not automatically a good. The misconception flags on this page are deliberately about a rule the learner applied, never about the learner.

5 · The measurement that does not exist

Persistent learner model vs stateless baseline
The controlled comparison that would justify all of the above.
QuestionEvidence
Does a tutor with a persistent learner model teach better than the same tutor without one? No controlled study located
Does switching scheduling algorithm (SM-2 → FSRS) improve any learning outcome? No controlled evidence. FSRS predicts recall better and, in simulation, buys the same knowledge for less time. Different claims.
Do high-frequency spaced-repetition users perform better? +4 to +13 points on USMLE Step 1 — all observational
Does knowledge tracing predict responses well? Yes, and that is a different question from whether it teaches
Read that table before you build this. Persistent state is the single most-assumed component of AI tutoring and, as far as this survey could establish, it has never been measured against a stateless baseline. Better prediction is not better teaching. A model that forecasts your next answer with high accuracy and changes nothing about what you are shown has bought you precisely nothing.

JushBJJ/Mr.-Ranedeer-AI-Tutor README.md, line 1: # DISCONTINUED

Mr. Ranedeer contained more pedagogical mechanism than most funded products: a learner configuration as a first-class object, a prerequisite ladder from 0.1 to 0.9, a 3/6/9-difficulty familiar/unfamiliar test, python-verified problems, a /test command. It tried to persist a learner model twice — once by base64-encoding it into the transcript, once as a file in the code-interpreter sandbox — and both attempts were requests to one vendor's model with one vendor's tool surface. When the tool surface moved, both decayed. Roughly half the stars of nanoGPT conferred no immunity whatsoever.

The rule it leaves behind. Never make a vendor behaviour load-bearing. A prompt is not a mechanism; it is a request for a mechanism. The record on this page is forty lines of JSON in a key you own, on hardware you own, in a format you can read without us — which is a much less impressive engineering achievement and a much more durable one.

What this proves / what it does not

What it proves
  • A learner model can be fully legible: every belief the system holds about you, in a format you can read, edit and contradict, with the evidence attached to each claim.
  • Deletion can be made real and provable — not a confirmation dialogue but a read-back showing the key returns null.
  • Persistence does not require a server, an account, or a vendor. One key, one JSON blob, one storage API that has not changed since 2009.
  • The parameters that drive the estimates can be exposed. Most systems hide them, which means the person being modelled cannot dispute the model, only its conclusions.
What it does not prove
  • That any of this helps anyone learn. See §5: the comparison has not been run. This demo makes the mechanism inspectable; it does not make it effective.
  • That BKT's estimates are accurate for you. The four parameters are defaults. Real deployments fit them to a population, and you may not be in it — which is exactly why they are editable here and, in most systems, are not.
  • That localStorage is an adequate answer for a real deployment. It is per-device, per-browser, silently cleared, and offers no audit trail. It is the right shape and the wrong infrastructure.
  • That legibility solves the § 300.624 problem. It solves the easy half. The hard half — influence already folded into weights or shared indices — is not solvable after the fact by any interface, including this one.
