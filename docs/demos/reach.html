A phone call beats the avatar

The instinct is that reach improves with bandwidth. Four measured arms say otherwise, and they say it with enough structure to be decomposed: voice call + SMS, +0.327 SD across five countries and N = 8,902, against SMS alone at +0.083, IVR at null, and live calls in Sierra Leone at −0.008. Below, the ingredients are separated and the richness term is solved for. It does not come out positive.

What this is

A channel comparator with an arithmetic spine. Four published effects, four ingredients, one exactly-determined linear system — solved live, then used to price channel combinations that nobody has run.

Why it matters

Most designs for reaching children who are not in the room start by specifying bandwidth: video, avatars, a 25 FPS face. The best-evidenced intervention in this whole literature is a text message and a person who rings at an agreed time. That is a standing rebuke, and the arithmetic below says where its power lives.

How to use it

Switch off targeting and leave everything else on. Whatever remains is what bandwidth and human presence are worth on their own. Then look at the badge on the readout: anything that is not one of the four measured arms is labelled an extrapolation, because it is one.

1 · Four arms, four ingredients, no spare parameters

Each measured arm is written as a vector of ingredients. Contact is worth one on every arm — something reached the learner. Human means a person was on the line. Targeting means the content was matched to what this learner could already do. Richness is a bandwidth score on the channel itself, from a text message at 0.05 to full video at 1.0.

2 · Build a channel

Toggle the ingredients. The prediction is the dot product of your vector with the coefficients above.

The one conclusion that survives the missing degrees of freedom. With four arms and four parameters the individual coefficients are an accounting identity, not an estimate — they have no standard errors because they have no residuals. But the sign of the richness term does not depend on the coding at all. IVR is a richer channel than SMS by any ordering anyone would defend, and IVR measured lower. So the richness coefficient is −0.083 / (rIVR − rSMS), which is negative for every positive gap between them. Move the richness slider on any arm and watch the sign hold.

3 · The wall the channel argument never reaches

Bandwidth is a solvable problem and it is the wrong one. The barrier that actually decides whether a child can be taught by a model is whether a model exists in a language they understand. These shares come from joining World Bank child-population data to a full multilingual benchmark table.

Read the third bar twice. For roughly 375 million children we cannot state whether a model works in their language, because nobody has measured it. That is not a capability gap. It is a measurement gap — and a measurement gap is the cheaper of the two to close. No learner-weighted multilingual benchmark exists anywhere: every benchmark weights languages by convenience or corpus size, never by how many children are sitting in a classroom being taught in them.

4 · And the part that is already tractable

The hopeful result, and it does not wait on frontier scale. A 0.55-billion-parameter fine-tuned encoder collapses the population of children whose languages score below 50 on the benchmark.

6 · What this proves, and what it licenses you to build

Established here
  • Bandwidth is not the mechanism, and that is provable from two arms. SMS beats IVR. Any richness ordering that puts voice above text forces the richness coefficient negative. No amount of parameter choice rescues it.
  • The winning arm's advantage is separable and cheap. Targeting solves to +0.335 SD — on its own, more than the entire measured effect of the best arm, because the richness of the phone call is charged against it. Every ingredient that matters is available to a programme with no video budget and no app.
  • Extrapolation is marked rather than hidden. The builder above reaches eight ingredient vectors at twenty-one richness settings. Four of those combinations correspond to something somebody actually ran. Every other readout carries a badge saying so, instead of quietly returning a number.
Conditions of use
  • Zero degrees of freedom is a real limitation. Four observations cannot identify four parameters and an error term. Treat the coefficients as a decomposition of what was measured, not as an estimate of what would happen. The robust claim is the sign of one of them, and it is stated above with its proof.
  • Ingredient additivity is an assumption. The survey's own reading is that the winning arm works through accountability and targeting together — a human at a scheduled time, delivering something matched to the learner. An additive model cannot see an interaction, so it charges the whole combined effect to whichever ingredient is unique to the winning arm. That is why targeting takes the credit here and human presence does not, and it is a property of the model rather than a finding about people.
  • Untargeted delivery is the failure mode, not the technology. Every null in this literature shares a structure: something was distributed and nothing was targeted. That is a design failure we know how to avoid, which raises the standard rather than lowering it — a system that widens gaps no longer has the excuse that everything does.
  • The language numbers are shares, not outcomes. "Has a functional model" is benchmark coverage above a threshold, not evidence that a child learns. And the third bar is the honest one: for 21.7% of the world's children the question has not been asked, so every optimistic sentence on this page is silent about them.
