Demo 07 · The Village

The Connector

The agent whose job is to get you away from the agent. It brokers contact with real people, weights meeting in person above meeting online because the measurement says to, and declines — explicitly, in words — to be your friend.

Computed: the matching Scripted: the refusal wording +3 months vs +5 in person
What this is

A role that brokers contact with real people and refuses to be your friend. It computes overlapping availability across time zones, ranks candidates, and declines a companionship framing outright.

Why it matters

Technology-mediated collaborative learning delivers +3 months against +5 in person — the mediation costs something real. And adults anthropomorphise AI companions more than teens do. A system that simulates friendship is substituting for the thing with the better evidence.

How to use it

Change a time zone or an availability window and watch the ranking reorder. Then toggle the EEF coefficient and see human contact move up the list. Read the refusal in §3 — it is scripted, and labelled as such.

HYBRID, and labelled part by part. §1 is COMPUTED — real interval arithmetic across time zones, a real scoring function, and the +5-vs-+3-months finding entering the arithmetic as an explicit coefficient you can see and switch off. §2 is SCRIPTED — the refusal sentences are written by hand, because generating them needs a model this page cannot ship; only the referral target inside them is computed. §3 is COMPUTED — a working policy checker you can throw sentences at.

The modality coefficient is not a taste. What the refusal is not. It is not a lecture about AI, and it is not a withdrawal. Every one of those replies ends by handing the learner to a specific named human at a specific time. A refusal that leaves someone with nothing is not a safeguard; it is an abandonment with a compliance justification attached. The measure of this component is not how firmly it says no — it is whether a human was actually reached.

Anthropomorphic bids for continued interaction are the failure mode. Type a candidate utterance, or press one of the samples, and the checker runs a real pattern set against it.

The uncomfortable pair. Row two says technology already costs you two months of progress on the one thing this agent is for. Row five says the adult building this product is, on the available evidence, more likely to anthropomorphise the thing than the teenager it is built for. Together they argue for an agent that is deliberately bad at being company and good at getting out of the way — and they argue against the intuition that the danger is confined to children.
And row four is the one people skip. EEF's Lesson Study trial — very high security, 6,437 pupils in the analysis — returned 0.02. Collaboration structures without content do nothing. A connector that successfully arranges a meeting with no shared task has, on the best available evidence, arranged nothing. Every proposal in §1 therefore carries a specific topic and a specific reciprocal exchange, not an invitation to “study together”.

What this proves / what it does not

What it proves
  • An effect size can be made load-bearing in code. The 3:5 ratio is a literal coefficient with a switch next to it, and flipping the switch visibly changes who gets proposed.
  • Brokering is a solvable scheduling problem: interval intersection across time zones, reciprocity as a scoring term, hard filters for safeguarding.
  • Refusal can be constructive — every scripted decline terminates in a named human and a real time slot rather than in a policy statement.
  • The anthropomorphic-bid prohibition is checkable by machine. The checker in §3 is crude, it is auditable, and it runs on every utterance rather than on a sample.
What it does not prove
  • That the refusal works. No learner is simulated here and none was tested. Whether a person in genuine isolation accepts a redirection to a stranger instead of the always-available agent is precisely the unmeasured question, and it is not a small one.
  • That the matching produces good pairs. It produces schedulable pairs with a plausible reciprocity story. Compatibility between humans is not an overlap calculation.
  • That +5 versus +3 transfers. Both figures carry LOW evidence security in the toolkit itself, and “months of progress” is a policy translation, not an effect size. The ratio is the best available anchor and it is a weak one.
  • That the pattern checker is adequate. It is a regex list. It will miss anything phrased obliquely, and a competent model asked to be warm will route around it in one attempt. It demonstrates that the constraint is expressible, not that it is enforceable.
