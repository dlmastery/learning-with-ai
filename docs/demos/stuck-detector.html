Demo 19 · The mechanism

Help that arrives before you ask

The measured failure point is not reaching the help. It is recognising you need it. The best-instrumented tutor in the world published a null traced to exactly that. This page builds the detector that removes the summoning step, using only signals a learner emits by acting, and then refuses to pretend the threshold is easy.

Detector, sweep and optimum all computed Deterministic seed, the same session every time The learner is simulated No affect inference, by law and by design
What this is

A running estimate of stuck-ness over one simulated session, computed from five behavioural signals and an intervention threshold you control. Underneath it, a ground-truth label the detector never sees: at each moment the learner is in flow, in productive struggle, or wheel-spinning.

Why it matters

Cognitive Tutor logs, twenty-five years ago: after three consecutive errors a hint was requested only 34% of the time, and 68% of hint levels were viewed for under one second. The help was there. It was not summoned, and when summoned it was not read. Khanmigo's published null is the same diagnosis, a quarter of a century later.

How to use it

There is one control that matters and it has no right setting. Slide the threshold down and the detector rescues wheel-spinners while interrupting people who were about to get there on their own; slide it up and the reverse. §5 puts the same threshold in front of two learners and shows it cannot be one number.

The tension this page must not dodge. Productive failure measures g = 0.36–0.58, and the finding is blunt: adding help to the struggle does not help. A system optimised to notice you are stuck and intervene is optimised to destroy the mechanism that produces that effect. The resolution proposed here is to intervene on the wrong kind of stuck. No trial has tested that resolution; it is a design claim, and the cost of a false interrupt on this page is set accordingly high.

2 · What the detector is allowed to see

Five signals, each one a by-product of the learner acting on the problem. Nothing is inferred about how they feel, and nothing is observed that they did not choose to emit.

SignalStatusWeightWhat it is
response_latencyused0.90 Seconds since the last action, standardised. Alone it means nothing, because long pauses are what productive struggle looks like too.
edit_backtrackused0.35 Deletions and reversals in the window. A learner rewriting is a learner still trying.
repeat_error_runused0.62 Consecutive identical error signatures. The load-bearing signal. Repetition without variation is what wheel-spinning is.
no_state_changeused0.22 Windows since the answer last changed, capped at ten. Separates thinking from stalling.
help_avoidanceused0.80 Help declined, or a hint opened and closed inside a second — the behaviour behind the 68% figure.
affect / frustrationrefused— Emotion inference in education is prohibited outright under EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) — a prohibition, not a risk tier. And nothing here needs it.
gaze / webcamrefused— Continuous biometric capture of a child, for a signal the behavioural set already carries.
keystroke dynamics as mood proxyrefused— Emotion inference in a behavioural costume. The prohibition is on the inference, not the sensor.

5 · Why the threshold cannot be one number

The same detector, the same threshold, two learners. Students in the bottom quintile of prerequisite knowledge wheel-spin 50% of the time against 10% for the top quintile. Half of a weak learner's session is spent going nowhere, for a reason that was fixed before the session started.

What this proves / what it does not

Read this as a list of who pays when each part is wrong.

ComponentStatus on this pageWho pays if it is wrong
The detector Real. A logistic sum over five declared features, smoothed, run on every window; every coefficient is in §2. Nobody yet. The weights are a design proposal no trial has fitted. In production a bad weight on repeat_error_run is the difference between the two failure modes.
The trade-off Real, and its shape is the finding: swept exhaustively, both curves counted at every threshold. No setting sends both to zero. The learner, either way. This is the structure of the problem and a better model does not remove it.
The learner Simulated from a seeded generator. The 50% / 10% rates are measured; how those states look in the signal stream is assumed. This page. If the two states are less separable in reality, every number in §3 is optimistic.
The latency-only comparison Honest but circular — it fails here because the generator gives productive struggle the longer pauses, which this page assumed rather than showed. Anyone reading it as evidence. It shows a single-signal detector can invert, not that it does.
The premise that arriving unasked helps Not shown, and there is a null against the adjacent claim. Carnegie Mellon built help-seeking support; help-seeking improved durably. Domain learning did not move. Everyone, including this design. Help-seeking was a necessary condition that turned out not to be sufficient: supplying it removes a blocker, it does not teach.
What survives that null. Invocation is part of the intervention — a tutor that must be summoned reaches the students who need it least, measured twice, two decades apart, by two organisations. What is not established is that removing the summoning step produces learning rather than merely producing more help. This design does not yet have that explanation.
