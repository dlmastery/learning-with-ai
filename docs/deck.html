Learning with AI · product thesis

Make polymathy ordinary.

A persistent personal faculty that can see the work, change the representation, remember what happened, and prove the learner can now act alone.

The structural failure

Education standardizes the three things that should be personal.

Where to begin

Prior knowledge varies far more than learning rate. A fixed syllabus begins too early for some learners and too late for others.

How to represent it

Text, speech, diagrams, manipulatives and worked examples are treated as content formats. They should be live instructional decisions.

What happens next

Teachers, tutors and apps lose the learner between sessions. Each new encounter pays the orientation cost again.

A learner can understand the idea and still fail the worksheet because decoding consumed the working memory the worksheet was meant to test. This is a core product requirement.

Prior-knowledge and learning-rate result: Koedinger et al., PNAS 2023. The learner story is an anonymized design vignette without a clinical claim.

The product

One closed loop from curiosity to retained capability.

ResumeRecall the open goal, constraints and last unresolved belief.

The interface can be voice, a shared canvas, a camera, a simulator or a page. The product is the control loop deciding what to do next—and what evidence would change that decision.

The personal faculty

Specialists propose. Evidence decides.

Diagnostician

Maintains competing explanations for the learner’s action and selects the next discriminating probe.

Domain mentor

Protects disciplinary correctness, prerequisite structure and authentic practice.

Representation director

Chooses text, speech, diagram, motion, code, model or simulation—and checks the result.

Access coach

Manages cognitive load, executive-function supports, communication modes and learner control.

The learner never manages an agent swarm. One accountable orchestrator exposes one next move, its reason and the evidence that would overturn it.

Certification requirement: each specialist needs scoped competence tests, abstention behavior, tool permissions and outcome monitoring. The “agent” label confers no qualification.

Capture why a technique works, when it fails and what learner evidence should invoke it, along with the media asset.

Publisher interchange

Authors retain provenance; concept packages can travel across books, courses and learner interfaces.

Learner ownership

State is inspectable, editable, exportable and revocable. A person can leave without losing their learning history.

The compounding loop

Outcome evidence improves the next decision.

What compounds

Belief libraries: which misconception predicted which action.

Representation policies: which medium helped which learner state.

Technique provenance: which creator move survived transfer.

Calibration: which tasks actually distinguish capability.

Noncompounding activity

Conversation volume, prompt collections and undifferentiated generated content.

A stronger frontier model should increase the value of the evidence layer.

The defensible asset is the map from learner evidence to a tested next action.

SELPA-first product requirements

Access is part of the control loop.

Multiple ways in

Speak, type, point, draw, select, photograph or manipulate. Keep reading ability from silently becoming the measure of every subject.

Multiple ways through

Control pace, voice, density, captions, contrast, motion and chunking. Preserve the concept while changing the interface.

Multiple ways out

Let learners demonstrate understanding in more than one mode, then verify the target skill directly when modality matters.

The first proof

Start where the outcome arrives from outside the product.

Recommended wedge

US SAT Math for learners with a dated test.

Bounded concepts, external scoring, a legible buyer, rapid creator distribution and repeated opportunities to test transfer.

The trial

Compare against the strongest widely available general model. Pre-register delayed, unassisted, novel items. Hold the final item bank outside the product team. Report subgroup gap change.

Founder decision required: confirm or replace this wedge before external circulation. TASK.md and the research corpus leave the first learner, buyer, geography and outcome unspecified. The recommendation is an editorial inference.

Business model

Begin with the learner. Expand through the content ecosystem.

1 · Direct

Outcome-bound membership for learners preparing for a dated external assessment. Price against coaching, with access accommodations included.

2 · Creator channel

Revenue share for authors and educators whose techniques become attributable, measured concept packages.

3 · Platform

Publisher and course-provider licensing for the compiler, learner-state interchange and outcome instrumentation.

The expansion path is learner → creator → publisher, with evidence at each transition.

Pricing, margin and acquisition assumptions require a founder-owned operating model before this slide becomes an investment claim.

Ecosystem strategy

Turn the best teaching moves into reusable, attributable infrastructure.

Authors and creators get

Provenance, distribution, outcome evidence, revenue participation, and a way to make techniques interactive without rebuilding them in every medium.

Learners get

A coherent experience across sources, portable history, access preferences that persist, and the ability to inspect why a source or technique was selected.

The system should expand the market for human expertise and preserve its attribution, agency and value.

The commitment

Make excellent personal instruction abundant—without making dependence abundant too.

The milestone is a learner completing a delayed, unassisted, novel task that was previously beyond reach, with the gap narrowing rather than widening.

18-month proof program

Ship one closed concept loop; validate the external assessment wedge; run a pre-registered comparison against a general model; establish the first creator and publisher interchange.
