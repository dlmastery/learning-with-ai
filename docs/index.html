Research and product direction

Personal instruction should not be scarce.

Frontier AI can already speak, see work, generate software and media, execute tools, remember across sessions and coordinate specialists. This project investigates how to turn those capabilities into durable learning for any student.

The opportunity

Give each learner the attention, range of explanations and continuity normally available only through a team of excellent tutors.

Start correctly

Find the actual starting point

Use short predictions, explanations, drawings and worked steps to locate prior knowledge and misconceptions before selecting material.

Explain precisely

Choose the right representation

Move among conversation, notation, diagrams, executable notebooks, animation, simulation and generated software while preserving the concept.

Stay responsive

Watch the work, then change course

Respond to the step where reasoning breaks. Persist when productive; pivot when evidence shows that the current method is failing.

Build independence

Separate help from mastery

Record what the learner can do after hints, tools and generated answers are removed. Schedule later retrieval instead of treating completion as learning.

Reduce friction

Supply executive function

Make starting obvious, break work into visible units, protect attention, recover after interruption and bring in a person when coordination is the real need.

Remain open

Connect the learning ecosystem

Use material from publishers, authors, teachers, courses and open resources while preserving rights, provenance and the learner's control over personal state.

A learner session

This is a product requirement, not a scripted demo. Each transition requires a real model capability, an inspectable state change and a measurable outcome.

ARRIVE

Resume without reconstruction

Restore the goal, previous work, unresolved question and next useful action.

DIAGNOSE

Ask for evidence of thinking

Use a task that distinguishes competing explanations of what the learner believes.

TEACH

Make the obstacle inspectable

Select an explanation or interactive medium tied to the obstacle, subject and access needs.

PRACTISE

Fade support

Move from guided attempts to an unfamiliar case completed without the earlier aid.

EXPLAIN

Require the learner to teach

Ask for a reason, counterexample or presentation that exposes remaining gaps.

RETURN

Revisit after time has passed

Use later, unassisted retrieval to decide what was learned and what needs another approach.

SELPA-first requirements

The system is designed around a learner who may understand the subject while decoding, working memory, attention or response format obscures that understanding.

Preserve the intellectual target

Change access, pacing and response mode without quietly reducing the concept.

Offer multiple ways to respond

Speech, drawing, pointing, manipulation, typing and formal notation should be available where valid.

Make time and state visible

Show what is happening now, what remains, what can be paused and how to restart.

Keep labels out of the inference loop

A diagnosis informs accommodations; it does not define a ceiling or replace observed work.

Measure subgroup effects

Average gains can conceal widening gaps. Access profiles belong in evaluation plans.

Escalate consequential decisions

IEPs, diagnoses and placement remain human team decisions with documented authority.

What has been measured

Build and research roadmap

The next work should retire uncertainty. Product milestones are paired with experiments that can show the proposed mechanism is unnecessary or wrong.

1

Delayed, unassisted transfer

Build the common outcome instrument first. Every later product comparison depends on it.

2

Persistent learner state

Compare memory-enabled and stateless tutors at matched model, time and compute.

3

Generated experience compiler

Render one concept as prose, diagram, notebook, app and simulation from the same verified concept specification.

4

Agent-role evaluations

Define tests for diagnosis, explanation, verification, access and routing before calling agents experts.

5

SELPA-first trial

Pre-register subgroup outcomes and compare response modes without lowering the target skill.

6

Creator and publisher protocol

Test concept packages carrying rights, provenance, author intent, calibrated items and attribution across partners.

The venture thesis

Frontier models will keep improving and remain broadly available. The durable asset is evidence about which action, representation and sequence helped a specific learner overcome a specific obstacle. The company earns that asset by measuring independent learning and by working across the content ecosystem.
