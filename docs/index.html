Capabilities that were structurally impossible — not expensive, impossible — because one adult cannot sit beside thirty children every hour for a decade.

Not the submitted answer. The attempt, the hesitation, the step where it went wrong, for every learner, every hour.

A whole faculty at once

Mentor, diagnostician, simulator, adversary, archivist. Three to five active in any hour. Every one of them is a person a rich family hires.

Now buildable

Credit that cannot be free-ridden

Cooperative learning's measured achievement effect turns out to be an incentive rule. Where the group reward is computed from every member's separately measured learning, the median is +0.32 across 64 studies, 50 of them significantly positive and none negative; where it rests on one shared product, +0.07. Rated observation across 49 classrooms found that condition present in 17% of lessons, because a teacher has to work it out by hand. A system already measuring every learner computes it every time.

Now buildable

The marks you are about to lose

A published mark scheme is per-item ground truth across millions of released questions. Run it backwards: before the learner sits a past paper, predict which marks go and why — knowledge gap, misread question, procedural slip, unfollowed convention, time — then mark the paper against the scheme. The prediction scores itself with no human in the loop, and a system that learned item-style tricks instead of a knowledge state should get worse at it.

The size of it

Learning is counted in opportunities. Almost none of a week supplies one.

Polymathy was never about intelligence

Across 1.3 million observations, learning rate varies by 1.14× between the 25th and 75th percentile. Prior knowledge varies by 3.6×. What limits how many fields you can enter is the cost of being a beginner: search, access and orientation. All attention. None of it talent.

Four minutes, or fifty-two

The same nominal school day yields either, depending on the cascade from allocated time to engaged time to time at the right difficulty. A thirteen-fold spread inside identical calendars. The explaining was never the scarce part.

A week's understanding in an hour

10–40× on the calendar, 3–5× on engaged effort, one documented case near 300×. Durability does not compress; a year-durable memory needs 18–36 day gaps. It is nearly free anyway: the same items, merely split.

The measurement nobody has run

No trial above carries a delayed, unassisted, novel-item outcome. Sierra Leone tested at endline with the tool still in the room. Kestin tested at the end of the hour. Tutor CoPilot did reach a distal outcome and came back null there. The frontier era has not met a ceiling; it has not yet run the study that could locate one. That is a hole in the record, and a very specific one, which is why the open problems are written as experiments anyone can run.

The same trial, two ways of taking the difference

Before any number on this page can be read, it has to say which difference it is. A pre-to-post gain carries learning, maturation, the testing effect and regression to the mean all summed together; the contrast with a control carries the treatment. One meta-analysis computed both on the same seven trials and printed both forest plots.

The seam opens again wherever anyone measures the item that was not taught. Automated writing evaluation — three studies from one group across six years, including a randomised one — improves the draft in front of the learner and leaves the next cold composition where it was. A week after an L2 study underlined every error in half the drafts, the group that had revised better wrote a fresh narrative and came back at g = −0.068. Dialogue-system practice transfers inside a modality and stops at its edge: speaking to speaking 0.84, written practice to speaking 0.29 [−0.21, 0.79]. Vocabulary instruction scores 0.50 on comprehension measures built from passages containing the taught words and 0.10 on a standardised test. Four literatures with no contact between them, one result: what a system taught and what a learner has are different quantities, and only one of the two is easy to instrument.

What separates a system that works from one that feels like it

Three results with a build consequence. They are why most of the field's effort has not landed, and each one is a constraint you can design against.

Felt learning and real learning move in opposite directions

Buljan 2018: three RCTs, 334 adults, an infographic against a plain-language summary. Preference moved d ≈ 0.48 and knowledge moved zero. Kaspar 2025 played a debiasing video first and the easiness effect survived it. No model was involved in either, which is why it carries: this is how people misjudge their own learning, so any system that optimises on ratings inherits it on day one.

A dashboard has to name the next move

Fuchs 1991: 33 teachers, three arms. One saw progress data. One saw the same data beside an expert system that named the next move. One saw neither. Both measuring arms changed their teaching more often; only the second lifted achievement. Human teachers, 1991 courseware, and still binding on a 2026 dashboard, because what was missing there was never compute. Streaks, mastery bars and adaptive difficulty all sit in that middle arm.

Unguarded assistance is an active harm

Bastani 2025, ~1,000 Turkish students on GPT-4, is the frontier-era result that matters most here. Later unassisted work came back 17% worse. Add guardrails and the unassisted coefficient is −0.004, not significant: harm gone, teaching not demonstrated. 109 citing works in fourteen months, and no replication with a withdrawal design. Anyone selling restraint as a learning gain is ahead of the evidence — including this page, in an earlier draft.

Instructional moves, ordered by measured effect. The specification this survey has been running on credit

One move that a system makes constantly is missing from that chart, because sorted by what was being sequenced, every instructional-sequencing experiment since the 1960s splits three ways. Scheduling inside a set of paired associates wins 11 of 14. Choosing which activity comes next for content already chosen wins 8 of 10. Ordering interdependent content over a prerequisite graph wins 0 of 8: six clean nulls and two mixed, three of the nulls on primary-school children doing arithmetic and fractions. That review closes in 2019, so every comparison inside it ran on a rule-based or reinforcement-learned sequencer. On a frontier one the experiment has never been run.

Our own ladder of explanation computes a mastery vector over a concept's transitive prerequisite closure and enters at the weakest link. That is the between-topic decision, the half with no positive result anywhere behind it, and the survey now says so in the paragraph that specifies it. The graph keeps its diagnostic job and loses its gate. The trial that would settle it randomises the graph instead of the traversal policy every previous study randomised: an equivalence design at a pre-registered margin of δ = 0.10 SD, roughly 857 learners across eight topic pairs, two terms of one mid-sized deployment. For the eleven-year-old this survey is written around, the answer decides between a tutor that teaches her photosynthesis and one that sends her back to fractions first.

Kindergarten to doctorate

The question this survey gets asked most: does any of this work the same way for a six-year-old and a PhD student? No, and the axis it varies along is not age.

The organising principle

Expertise reversal. The same instructional support measures d = +0.505 for novices and d = −0.428 for experts. Not "less useful" — actively harmful. Worked examples, explicit guidance and scaffolding are the strongest tools we have for beginners, and become obstacles for people who already know the domain.

So the K-12 → postgraduate span is a reversal, and not a difficulty gradient. It runs per topic: the same learner is a novice in one chapter and an expert in the next. A system that scales instruction up as the learner advances has it exactly backwards.

What the tutor's job actually is, from before school to mid-career

Under 5

Ship nothing, then ship one shape

Across 122 independent effect sizes covering ages 0–6, a screen costs about half a standard deviation against the same content from a person, with no difference between live and prerecorded video. Responsiveness does not rescue it: in a four-condition trial of 132 toddlers, neither the responsive nor the unresponsive video condition taught anything. What moves is the adult, at d = 1.01 on caregiver book-sharing competence across 19 RCTs. From three to five the supported surface is a dialogic reading partner with the caregiver in the room, and the equivalence claim under it is one underpowered interaction at n = 117.

K–5

Be the thing that cannot be argued with

Explicit, systematic, high-dosage, and above all consistent. The object refuses illegal states; the child is wrong in private, repeatedly, without anyone's face changing. Withholding does damage here; this is where the Socratic instinct is wrong.

6–12

Fade the scaffold when the evidence says to

The band where the reversal actually happens, so it is the band where measuring prior knowledge per topic becomes load-bearing. Fading too early and fading too late are both failures, and they look the same from the outside.

Undergraduate

Stop explaining. Start interrogating.

Guidance now costs more than it gives. The high-value moves become productive failure, unannounced objection, and problems that have never existed before. Explanation becomes something the learner produces.

Postgraduate

The learner knows more than the tutor

Inside their niche the model is not the expert, and pretending otherwise is the failure mode. Its job inverts completely: adversary, verifier, literature memory, connector to humans. It must be useful without being right.

Adults

The one outcome somebody else records

Adults hold the only unambiguous transfer criterion anywhere in this survey: a job, recorded by somebody else in state wage records. The training industry measures learning 234 times and results 26, on 1,748 people in total, which is the whole evidence base for whether any of it changes something a payer wanted. Dosage comes before efficacy here — the one randomised attempt to pay adult literacy learners for turning up reduced attendance by about 1.5 sessions.

What to build, and what to stop building

Every result here carries a direct consequence for a product decision, and about a third of them arrived from the subject literatures the survey opened last: the relationship, the examination, anxiety, writing, second language.

Kills a product

The exit ticket is not the outcome

Tutor CoPilot, 2024, a frontier LLM behind 900 human tutors, moved its in-product exit ticket +4pp and returned a null on the end-of-year test. That dissociation between the proxy a product can see and the outcome anyone cares about is the most reproducible finding in this literature, and it predates LLMs by a decade.

Free fix

Say it before they study

Teaching with a prior expectancy: g = 0.48, k = 39, human tutees. Without: g = −0.02. "Now explain it back to me," asked at the end, is the null condition, and is how nearly every product ships it. Ordering one sentence is free, and no LLM study has tested it.

Inverts the ask

The audience is a net cost

Teaching to a camera beat teaching to one student and to seven — on transfer, with lower pulse rate, lower anxiety, lower load and more idea units. Presenting to a room is a barrier that buys nothing.

Must-fix bug

Vision models erase the mistakes

Asked to transcribe student work, VLMs silently repair the errors — the sign, the paren, the missing step. The mistakes are the entire reason for looking at the page.

Boundary

A scheduler is not a curriculum

The sequencing split above is half of this. The other half is that sophistication inside the schedule has not paid either: Duolingo's own scheduler against plain Leitner, on roughly a million students, returned −7.3% practice.

Backfire

Announced devil's advocate

Saying "let me play devil's advocate" produces cognitive bolstering of the learner's original view. The exit is unannounced, model-owned objection.

Fairness

Detectors are conventionality meters

61.22% false-positive on non-native writers versus 5.19% native. Students taught sentence frames as an accommodation are maximally exposed, because the accommodation lowers perplexity by design.

Objective

The Null-Learner Test

Simulate an agent maximising your metric while teaching nothing. DAU, streaks, XP and time-on-app all pass, so all are disqualified as objectives.

Reach

A scheduled call, on any phone

Human-delivered voice call + SMS: +0.327 SD across 5 countries, N = 8,902. SMS alone: +0.083, null in three. What carries it is someone on the line at an appointed time, which is the one thing a model can now supply on every phone at no marginal cost. Bandwidth is doing none of the work.

Frontier

Real-time is solved

640×368 at 25 FPS, ~200 ms model-side latency: shorter than the pause between two people in conversation. The world persists, and events arrive on a stream of their own.

Empty chair

Zero RCTs on disabled learners

ERIC + Europe PMC, censused twice: 30 GenAI-education RCTs mention students, 0 mention disability. Every effect size in this field was measured on somebody else's child.

Backfire

A warmth setting raises the error rate

Five models fine-tuned on 1,617 conversations to answer more warmly, factual content held constant: error rates rose on every evaluation, mean +7.43 pp, and agreement with a user's incorrect belief rose +11 pp. Worst where a struggling learner actually lives — where the user's message expressed sadness the gap widened to +11.9 pp against a 6.8 pp baseline. MMLU and GSM8K barely moved, so the benchmarks a tutoring team runs do not see any of it.

Two channels

Anxiety is not a knowledge gap

Of 1,757 children screened, 77% of those with high maths anxiety had typical or high mathematics performance. In samples selected for low maths ability the anxiety–achievement correlation falls to r = −.09 against −.28 elsewhere. Read distress as a knowledge gap and you over-remediate three quarters of the anxious children you meet; read a right answer as comfort and you miss the child who is fluent and frightened.

Must-fix bug

Better speech recognition erases the error

On phoneme-level mispronunciation detection, Whisper Small beats Medium beats Large with the same model behind it, and the authors say why: a stronger recogniser repairs the learner's pronunciation while transcribing it, since recovering the intended word is its entire objective. The best end-to-end frontier audio model recovers 41.3% of annotated errors and is right about 52.7% of the ones it flags. The upgrade path runs backwards.

Free fix

Withhold the form

Shown a wrong sentence, a model's reflex is to say it back correctly. That is a recast, which is 57% of the corrective feedback teachers already supply against 30% for prompts, and the least supported of the three types: recasts 0.53, prompts 0.83, explicit correction 0.84 across 15 classroom studies. Prompting self-repair recruits the generation effect and costs one line of a system prompt.

Market

Advertised at three to seven times measured

Seven estimators run against a stratified sample of about 6,700 SAT registrants put commercial coaching at 6–8 scale points on verbal and 13–18 on mathematics, or roughly one further question correct per eight hours of effort. The two largest firms advertised 120 and 140 combined. The free alternative's famous 115-point figure traces to a press release; its own technical report, controlling for prior score, puts six hours of use at +21 points.

Objective

Delete the pond, then leave it deleted

School-average achievement depresses a student's academic self-concept at β = −0.28 across 1.28 million students, and a separate cross-national test found it negative in all 26 countries. A one-to-one tutor removes the comparison class by construction and puts it straight back the moment it ships percentiles, leaderboards or "students like you". Nobody has yet measured academic self-concept in a learner using an AI tutor.

Time-sensitive · verified 28 Jul 2026

The EU AI Act's Annex III education obligations were deferred to 2 December 2027 by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI, in force 27 July 2026), verified against the EUR-Lex primary text. But Article 50 (chatbot disclosure and synthetic-content marking) still applies from 2 August 2026. For a conversational tutor that is the live deadline. Two sources a reasonable person would check still give the wrong answer: artificialintelligenceact.eu is stamped "last updated 1 August 2024," and the Commission's own Digital Omnibus page still describes only the proposal.

Where we are still in the dark

Not open problems: those are catalogued and specified with experiments. These are the places where the whole frame may be wrong, and where nobody is currently looking.

Correlated pedagogical error at population scale

A human teacher's blind spots are idiosyncratic — thirty teachers make thirty different mistakes and the system averages them out. A model's blind spots are systematic and identical for every learner simultaneously. Nobody has studied what happens when ten million children are taught the same subtle misconception on the same afternoon. There is no monitoring for it and no name for it.

Every effect we have is measured in weeks

The longest outcome in this corpus is months; most are a single session. A technique that measures +0.5 SD at six weeks and quietly corrodes a learner's willingness to struggle over three years would look identical in every study we cite. We are optimising on a horizon far shorter than the thing we claim to affect.

What the model teaches implicitly, every single turn

Every interaction teaches epistemics: what counts as an answer, how much confidence is appropriate, whether to defer, when a question is finished. That curriculum runs constantly, is measured nowhere, and is plausibly larger than the subject-matter effect.

The learner model may be a category error

We assume a stable person to be modelled. In childhood the subject changes faster than the model converges, and a good fit to who a child was in October may be an active constraint on who they are becoming by March. Nobody has asked whether personalisation is developmentally anti-adaptive.

Perfect personalisation may delete the only measured route to working with a person

Two meta-analyses of scripted collaboration split the same way. On domain knowledge they return about a fifth of a standard deviation, d = 0.20 and g = 0.24. On collaboration skill they return d = 0.95 and g = 0.72, and nothing else in this survey moves that second outcome at all. The part of a group a tutor cannot supply — two people who have each committed to a position and neither of whom knows the answer — is worth perhaps 0.1 to 0.2 SD on achievement and is the only part that cannot be faked. This document has treated personalisation as an unmixed good and never once costed that.

Every study is one classroom in a world where nobody else has it

Education is heavily positional: credentials, ranking, admission. Every effect size quoted here was measured where the treatment group had something the others did not. What the same technique does when everyone has it is a different question, not a smaller version of that result, and it has never been asked.

We cannot tell competence from performance any more

Every assessment instrument in existence assumes the artifact reflects the person who submitted it. That assumption broke. The field's response has been detection, which runs 61.22% false-positive on non-native writers. Treating a measurement crisis as a cheating problem may be the wrong frame entirely.

Nobody has checked whether a wrong-on-purpose agent is safe

The teachable agent is this survey's most promising untested mechanism, and it requires an agent that holds an error without self-correcting. No one has measured whether learners repair the error or internalise it. The failure mode is obvious in hindsight and entirely unstudied.

375 million children are unmeasured — and that number is itself an artifact

We can state that 21.7% of the world's children have no benchmark coverage in any of their languages. What we cannot state is what happens to a language when its children are taught through a model that speaks it badly: whether that accelerates a shift already underway, and on what timescale.

How to tell a real claim from a good demo

Aimed at any vendor, any paper, any demo — and at this document. Most claims in this field fail on the first three.

Show me the delayed, unassisted, novel-item test.

Not the practice score. Not the same items. Weeks later, device closed, problems never seen before. ERIC returns 0 records for "retention test" AND "ChatGPT", and 0 for "transfer test". Without a delayed unassisted outcome, nothing was measured about learning.

Within-group, or against a control?

A pre-to-post gain carries maturation, practice, the testing effect and regression to the mean along with the treatment. In one trial reported both ways the two estimates sit 2.30 SD apart: d = 2.35 within-group, d = 0.05 against the control, same learners. An unlabelled effect size in a product claim is almost always the first one.

What happened to the gap, not the mean?

Report the interaction with prior attainment. An intervention with a healthy mean that loads on already-strong learners has made things worse. Untargeted delivery reliably does this: Sierra Leone loaded at +0.195 SD per SD of baseline. Prior attainment is not the only axis that sorts them, either. An adaptive trainer for 67 children with dyscalculia worked, and the gain concentrated in the children with low maths anxiety and no additional reading or spelling disorder. A subgroup split at that sample size is a warning and never a result, and it is the warning aimed straight at this project's premise.

Does your metric survive the Null-Learner Test?

Simulate an agent that maximises it while teaching nothing. If the metric cannot tell the difference, it is not an objective. Engagement, time-on-task, streaks, satisfaction and session count all fail.

Which arm isolates the AI from the humans around it?

Both real outcome trials of the best-resourced AI tutor wrapped it in human structure doing visible work. In one, 44.3% of tutor edits were slowing the AI's questioning down. No published trial isolates prompt-alone pedagogy from tablets, pair-work and teacher lesson design.

Is that a backtest or an intervention?

Log-loss on review histories is not a learning outcome. On a 350-million-review benchmark a zero-parameter moving average beats every FSRS version. Predicting what a learner already did is a different quantity from causing them to remember.

What is your inter-rater reliability on the thing you claim to measure?

Published once for the field's leading pedagogy rubric and never again: Krippendorff's α of 0.066 for "inspires interest" and 0.023 for "monitors motivation". Two of five principles rest on constructs experts cannot reliably identify. Human graders of code reach α ≈ 0.20.

Active control, or nothing?

Against "no intervention", almost everything works. Orton-Gillingham looks excellent until it is compared with other explicit instruction, where it is g = 0.22, p = .40. The robot meta-analysis spans +0.75 versus nothing to −0.06 versus a teacher.

What did you pre-register, and what did you report?

The largest AI-tutoring trial in this corpus abandoned its pre-registered subdomain analyses, had differential attrition favouring treatment, and swapped models mid-trial. All three are visible only in the appendix.

Who consented out?

Every result is conditioned on a connected, consenting, mostly curious population. The learners this field most claims to serve are the ones least likely to be in the sample — and in one census, none of them are.

What would falsify your thesis — and has it already happened?

The falsifier we accept is an active control: a plain frontier chatbot, run against the assembled system on a delayed, unassisted, novel-item test. If the mechanisms do not beat that arm, they are decorative and the model was doing the work. Fütterer 2026 has already run that comparison against a lighter set of mechanisms, n = 371, and found no advantage on knowledge, effort or strategy use. Gu & Yan put LLM tutoring at g = 0.077 without teacher support. The best-powered frontier studies on record are nulls, and our own evidence repeatedly shows elaboration losing to simplicity: five explanation rungs did not beat three (p = .738), multiple representations harmed, mixed agent panels lost to one good agent.
