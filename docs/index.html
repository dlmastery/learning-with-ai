A tutor producing 1.2–1.7 years of maths progress in eight weeks, in one of the world's most resource-constrained school systems, is plausibly the strongest evidence in the history of educational technology. It happened in June 2026.

The finding

Restraint is the active ingredient. Everything else is delivery.

The same model, given to the same students, produces opposite outcomes depending on one property:

ConditionPracticeExam, unassisted
Answers freely+48%−17%
Withholds · hints · requires reasoning+127%no penalty

This is good news and it is immediately actionable: the win needs no new model, no new capability, and no extra cost. It needs a loop that decides when not to answer.

Why the field mostly builds the other one

A measurement problem with a fixable cause.

Felt learning is what every optimisation loop can measure. Real learning is what none of them measure. So systems drift toward the former — and the two dissociate:

CapabilityFelt / affectActually learned
AI assistancepractice +48%unassisted −17%
Pedagogical avatarsaffect d = .85–1.01did not move
Animation vs staticcomprehensibility, interest, enjoyment ↑“but not comprehension test score”
Active learning (inverted)felt they learned lesslearned more

RLHF optimises for preferred responses — fluent, complete, immediate — which is precisely the fluency-illusion profile. And every LLM→explanatory-video pipeline surveyed optimises on VLM or human preference judgments, i.e. directly on the axis that dissociates from comprehension.

Findings that change what you build

Verified

Kimi K3 is not a world model

Release blog never uses “world model”, “simulation”, “physics” or “environment”. It writes code and iterates on screenshots. The showcased world shipped as a 19-second video with no playable artifact.

Measured

Generated worlds get physics wrong

VideoPhy best case 39.6% physical adherence; hard subset 22%. Physics-IQ: understanding is “unrelated to visual realism.” Photorealism makes the error more persuasive.

Correction

Expanding intervals don’t work

g = 0.034, n.s. The expansion curve in SM-2 / FSRS is not the active ingredient. Scheduling retrieval is (classroom d = 0.54).

Demolished

Bloom’s 2σ

Two dissertations, three-week units, author-written narrow tests. Across 96 randomized tutoring studies, none reproduced 2σ. Pooled ES = 0.288.

Replication

Knowledge tracing hit its ceiling in 2011

DKT’s result was inflated by 23.6% duplicated rows. Cleaned, PFA matches it. SAKT fails replication on all nine datasets.

Unlock

The accuracy ceiling is a privacy gift

If a 21-parameter model is at the ceiling, the learner model runs on-device. No cloud, no transmission, no vendor holding a child’s cognitive profile.

Blocker

A competent AI cannot be taught

Teaching ChatGPT fails to build error-correction skill — the model cannot stay wrong. Sycophancy: 58.19% capitulation, 14.66% regressive.

Backfire

Role-played devil’s advocate

Announcing it produces cognitive bolstering of the learner’s original view. The exit: unannounced, model-owned objection — not pretending to disagree.

Equity

Unconstrained AI is regressive

No main effect overall — but a widened gap between low- and high-prior-knowledge students. It helps those who already know how to drive it.

The architecture

JARVIS is the right image and the wrong specification.

JARVIS assists an expert. A tutor must build one. Every behaviour correct for an expert operator — answer instantly, never withhold, comply — is wrong for a learner. So the pedagogical JARVIS is defined by a capability the fictional one never needs: the judgment to refuse.

Real-time substrate is no longer the blocker. Wan Streamer v0.2/v0.3 runs 640×368 at 25 FPS with ~200 ms model-side latency — inside the human turn-gap budget of 100–200 ms — and v0.3’s world + event stream decomposition maps cleanly onto pedagogy: the world is the lesson context, the event stream is the teaching.

Editorial standard

Every claim carries a source URL and an evidence label — MEASURED-RCT, MEASURED-META, MEASURED-BENCH, OBSERVED, VENDOR, DEMO, INFERENCE. A VENDOR claim may never be restated as a finding. Every section must contain at least one documented negative or null result. Effect sizes over adjectives.
