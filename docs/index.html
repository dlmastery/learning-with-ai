Kindergarten to doctorate

The question this survey gets asked most: does any of this work the same way for a six-year-old and a PhD student? No — and the axis it varies along is not age.

The organising principle

Expertise reversal. The same instructional support measures d = +0.505 for novices and d = −0.428 for experts. Not "less useful" — actively harmful. Worked examples, explicit guidance and scaffolding are the strongest tools we have for beginners, and become obstacles for people who already know the domain.

So the K-12 → postgraduate span is not a difficulty gradient. It is a reversal, and it runs per-topic rather than per-person: the same learner is a novice in one chapter and an expert in the next. A system that scales instruction up as the learner advances has it exactly backwards.

What the tutor's job actually is, at each end

K–5

Be the thing that cannot be argued with

Explicit, systematic, high-dosage, and above all consistent. The object refuses illegal states; the child is wrong in private, repeatedly, without anyone's face changing. Withholding does damage here — this is where the Socratic instinct is wrong.

6–12

Fade the scaffold on evidence, not schedule

The band where the reversal actually happens, so it is the band where measuring prior knowledge per topic becomes load-bearing. Fading too early and fading too late are both failures, and they look the same from the outside.

Undergraduate

Stop explaining. Start interrogating.

Guidance now costs more than it gives. The high-value moves become productive failure, unannounced objection, and problems that have never existed before. Explanation becomes something the learner produces.

Postgraduate

The learner knows more than the tutor

Inside their niche the model is not the expert, and pretending otherwise is the failure mode. Its job inverts completely: adversary, verifier, literature memory, connector to humans. It must be useful without being right.

Findings that change what you build

Kills a product

Measurement without a rule is inert

Both arms revised programs more often; only the arm told what to change moved achievement. Dashboards, streaks, mastery bars and adaptive difficulty are all the arm that measured more and moved nothing.

Free fix

Say it before they study

Teaching with a prior expectancy: g = 0.48. Without: g = −0.02. "Now explain it back to me," asked at the end, is the null condition — and is how nearly every product ships it.

Inverts the ask

The audience is a net cost

Teaching to a camera beat teaching to one student and to seven — on transfer, with lower pulse rate, lower anxiety, lower load and more idea units. Presenting to a room is a barrier that buys nothing.

Must-fix bug

Vision models erase the mistakes

Asked to transcribe student work, VLMs silently repair the errors — the sign, the paren, the missing step. The mistakes are the entire reason for looking at the page.

Boundary

Scheduling fails on structure

Adaptive scheduling: 11 of 14 trials significant on flashcard-type material, 0 of 8 on interdependent content. Duolingo's scheduler vs plain Leitner on ~1M students: −7.3% practice.

Ceiling

Knowledge tracing is exhausted

An untrained LSTM is within 0.03 AUC of the trained one. An oracle that knows the exact moment of learning beats simple logistic PFA by 0.002. The headroom is not in the model.

Backfire

Announced devil's advocate

Saying "let me play devil's advocate" produces cognitive bolstering of the learner's original view. The exit is unannounced, model-owned objection — not performed disagreement.

Fairness

Detectors are conventionality meters

61.22% false-positive on non-native writers versus 5.19% native. Students taught sentence frames as an accommodation are maximally exposed, because the accommodation lowers perplexity by design.

Objective

The Null-Learner Test

Simulate an agent maximising your metric while teaching nothing. DAU, streaks, XP and time-on-app all pass — so all are disqualified as objectives.

Reach

A phone call beats the avatar

Voice call + SMS: +0.327 SD across 5 countries, N = 8,902. SMS alone: +0.083, null in three. The mechanism is a human on the line at a scheduled time, not the bandwidth.

Frontier

Real-time is solved

640×368 at 25 FPS with ~200 ms model-side latency — inside the human conversational turn-gap — with a persistent world and a separate event stream.

Empty chair

Zero RCTs on disabled learners

ERIC + Europe PMC, censused twice: 30 GenAI-education RCTs mention students, 0 mention disability. Every effect size in this field was measured on somebody else's child.

The unknown unknowns

Not open problems — those are catalogued and specified with experiments. These are the places where the whole frame may be wrong, and where nobody is currently looking.

Correlated pedagogical error at population scale

A human teacher's blind spots are idiosyncratic — thirty teachers make thirty different mistakes and the system averages them out. A model's blind spots are systematic and identical for every learner simultaneously. Nobody has studied what happens when ten million children are taught the same subtle misconception on the same afternoon. There is no monitoring for it and no name for it.

Every effect we have is measured in weeks

The longest outcome in this corpus is months; most are a single session. A technique that measures +0.5 SD at six weeks and quietly corrodes a learner's willingness to struggle over three years would look identical in every study we cite. We are optimising on a horizon far shorter than the thing we claim to affect.

What the model teaches implicitly, every single turn

Every interaction teaches epistemics — what counts as an answer, how much confidence is appropriate, whether to defer, when a question is finished. That curriculum runs constantly, is measured nowhere, and is plausibly larger than the subject-matter effect.

The learner model may be a category error

We assume a stable person to be modelled. In childhood the subject changes faster than the model converges, and a good fit to who a child was in October may be an active constraint on who they are becoming by March. Nobody has asked whether personalisation is developmentally anti-adaptive.

Every study is one classroom in a world where nobody else has it

Education is heavily positional — credentials, ranking, admission. Every effect size quoted here was measured where the treatment group had something the others did not. What the same technique does when everyone has it is not a smaller version of that result. It is a different question, and it has never been asked.

We cannot tell competence from performance any more

Every assessment instrument in existence assumes the artifact reflects the person who submitted it. That assumption broke. The field's response has been detection — which runs 61.22% false-positive on non-native writers. Treating a measurement crisis as a cheating problem may be the wrong frame entirely.

Nobody has checked whether a wrong-on-purpose agent is safe

The teachable agent is this survey's most promising untested mechanism, and it requires an agent that holds an error without self-correcting. No one has measured whether learners repair the error or internalise it. The failure mode is obvious in hindsight and entirely unstudied.

375 million children are unmeasured — and that number is itself an artifact

We can state that 21.7% of the world's children have no benchmark coverage in any of their languages. What we cannot state is what happens to a language when its children are taught through a model that speaks it badly — whether that accelerates a shift already underway, and on what timescale.

The questions worth asking

Aimed at any vendor, any paper, any demo — and at this document. Most claims in this field fail on the first three.

Show me the delayed, unassisted, novel-item test.

Not the practice score. Not the same items. Weeks later, device closed, problems never seen before. ERIC returns 0 records for "retention test" AND "ChatGPT", and 0 for "transfer test". Without a delayed unassisted outcome, nothing was measured about learning.

What happened to the gap, not the mean?

Report the interaction with prior attainment. An intervention with a healthy mean that loads on already-strong learners has made things worse. Untargeted delivery reliably does this — Sierra Leone loaded at +0.195 SD per SD of baseline.

Does your metric survive the Null-Learner Test?

Simulate an agent that maximises it while teaching nothing. If the metric cannot tell the difference, it is not an objective. Engagement, time-on-task, streaks, satisfaction and session count all fail.

Which arm isolates the AI from the humans around it?

Both real outcome trials of the best-resourced AI tutor wrapped it in human structure doing visible work — in one, 44.3% of tutor edits were slowing the AI's questioning down. No published trial isolates prompt-alone pedagogy from tablets, pair-work and teacher lesson design.

Is that a backtest or an intervention?

Log-loss on review histories is not a learning outcome. On a 350-million-review benchmark a zero-parameter moving average beats every FSRS version. Predicting what a learner already did is a different quantity from causing them to remember.

What is your inter-rater reliability on the thing you claim to measure?

Published once for the field's leading pedagogy rubric and never again: Krippendorff's α of 0.066 for "inspires interest" and 0.023 for "monitors motivation". Two of five principles rest on constructs experts cannot reliably identify. Human graders of code reach α ≈ 0.20.

Active control, or nothing?

Against "no intervention", almost everything works. Orton-Gillingham looks excellent until it is compared with other explicit instruction, where it is g = 0.22, p = .40. The robot meta-analysis spans +0.75 versus nothing to −0.06 versus a teacher.

What did you pre-register, and what did you report?

The largest AI-tutoring trial in this corpus abandoned its pre-registered subdomain analyses, had differential attrition favouring treatment, and swapped models mid-trial. All three are visible only in the appendix.

Who consented out?

Every result is conditioned on a connected, consenting, mostly curious population. The learners this field most claims to serve are the ones least likely to be in the sample — and in one census, none of them are.

What would falsify your thesis — and has it already happened?

The honest counter-case to this whole survey: 0.2–0.4 SD may be a population parameter rather than a technology limit. The best-powered studies on record are nulls. And our own evidence repeatedly shows elaboration losing to simplicity — five explanation rungs did not beat three (p = .738), multiple representations harmed, mixed agent panels lost to one good agent.

Editorial standard

Every claim carries a source and an evidence label — MEASURED-RCT, MEASURED-META, MEASURED-BENCH, OBSERVED, VENDOR, DEMO, INFERENCE. A VENDOR claim is never restated as a finding. Every section carries at least one documented null. Unverifiable claims are reported as unverifiable — never laundered, never omitted. Effect sizes over adjectives.
