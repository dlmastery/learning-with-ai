Findings that change what you build

Verified

Kimi K3 is not a world model

Its release blog never says “world model,” “simulation,” “physics” or “environment.” It writes code and iterates on screenshots. The showcased world shipped as a 19-second video with no playable artifact.

Measured

Generated worlds get physics wrong

VideoPhy best case 39.6% adherence; hard subset 22%. Physics-IQ: understanding is “unrelated to visual realism.” Photorealism makes the error more persuasive.

Unlock

The accuracy ceiling is a privacy gift

Knowledge tracing peaked around 2011 — DKT’s result was inflated by 23.6% duplicate rows; PFA matches it cleaned. If 21 parameters is the ceiling, the learner model runs on-device.

Blocker

A competent AI cannot be taught

Teaching ChatGPT fails to build error-correction skill — the model cannot stay wrong. Sycophancy: 58.19% capitulation, 14.66% of it toward wrong answers.

Backfire

Role-played devil’s advocate

Announcing it produces cognitive bolstering of the learner’s original view. The exit is unannounced, model-owned objection — not pretending to disagree.

Equity

Unconstrained AI is regressive

No main effect overall — but a widened gap between low- and high-prior-knowledge students. It helps those who already know how to drive it.

Fairness

Detectors are conventionality meters

61.22% false-positive on non-native writers vs 5.19% native. Students taught formulaic structures as an accommodation are maximally exposed.

Objective

The Null-Learner Test

Simulate an agent maximising your metric while teaching nothing. DAU, streaks, XP and time-on-app all pass — so all are disqualified as objectives.

Substrate

Real-time is solved

Wan Streamer v0.3: 640×368 @ 25 FPS, ~200 ms model-side latency — inside the human turn-gap budget — with a persistent world plus a separate event stream.

The thesis under test

Cost, not efficacy

Most great pedagogical systems were abandoned for cost, not evidence. The Oxbridge tutorial, Socratic dialogue, apprenticeship, chavruta, the Keller Plan, one-to-one mastery learning — all worked, all were labour-intensive, all lost to economics.

So the question is not “what new pedagogy does AI enable?” but “which known-good pedagogy just became affordable?” — and, critically, which mechanisms survive having the human replaced. Chavruta needs a genuine peer. A tutorial needs an expert who can be wrong and argued with. Some do not survive.

Editorial standard

Every claim carries a source URL and an evidence label — MEASURED-RCT, MEASURED-META, MEASURED-BENCH, OBSERVED, VENDOR, DEMO, INFERENCE. A VENDOR claim may never be restated as a finding. Every section must contain at least one documented negative or null result. Effect sizes over adjectives.
