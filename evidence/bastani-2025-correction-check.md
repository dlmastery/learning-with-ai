# Verification note — the PNAS "Correction" to Bastani et al. 2025

**Date checked:** 2026-07-27 · **Checked by:** primary source, Europe PMC full text
**Status:** the −17% finding STANDS. Do not re-open this.

## Why this note exists

Report G3 flagged: *"Bastani's −17% now carries a PNAS Correction, a design
critique, and a counter-study. It's load-bearing in five of our sections and must
not be cited as settled."* The −17% appears in `survey/01-central-finding.md`
(×4), `survey/03-the-vision.md`, and `docs/index.html` (×2). It was verified
rather than accepted.

## What the correction actually says

- **Original:** `10.1073/pnas.2422633122`, PNAS 122(26), 25 Jun 2025
- **Correction:** `10.1073/pnas.2518204122`, PNAS 122(34), 20 Aug 2025, PMID
  40833419, PMCID PMC12403119. Crossref links it with `update-type: correction`.

Full text, retrieved from Europe PMC (`MEASURED` — primary document):

> "The authors note that, **due to a production error, the affiliation for Osbert
> Bastani appeared incorrectly.** The affiliation for Osbert Bastani should
> instead appear as 'Department of Computer and Information Science, School of
> Engineering and Applied Science, University of Pennsylvania…'"

That is the entire content. **No datum, estimate, figure, table, sample size, or
conclusion is altered.** The +48% during-access gain and the −17% unassisted-exam
penalty for the unguarded GPT Base arm are untouched.

## The separate items G3 conflated with it

A "Correction" in the Crossref/PubMed sense is a formal erratum by the publisher.
It is not a challenge to findings, and the two must not be merged. Genuinely
distinct and still open:

- **`10.2139/ssrn.4898213` — "Critique of Generative AI Can Harm Learning Study
  Design"** (SSRN). A third-party methodological critique, not a publisher
  erratum. Unrefereed preprint; weigh accordingly. Worth reading on its merits.
- G3 also refers to a counter-study. **Unverified in this session** — no DOI was
  supplied. Flagged for follow-up; not to be cited until identified.

## Standing rule this establishes

A subagent's characterisation of a source is a *lead*, not a finding. Any claim
that a load-bearing number has been corrected, retracted, or superseded is
verified against the publisher record — Crossref `update-to` / `updated-by`, then
the erratum's own text — before a single sentence of the survey is changed.
