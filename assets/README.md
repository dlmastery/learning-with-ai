# Visual system

Every survey chapter receives at least one explanatory visual. Visuals have two
classes:

1. **Editorial illustrations** under `illustrations/`: generated bitmap scenes
   used for the cover, section openings, and human stories.
2. **Technical diagrams** under `diagrams/`: deterministic SVG files with exact
   labels, accessible contrast, source notes, and meaningful alt text.

## House style

- Optimistic, humane, and globally representative.
- Learners act, build, speak, calculate, and collaborate; they are not passive
  recipients.
- Teachers and families are amplified rather than replaced.
- AI appears as responsive infrastructure and expert support, not a humanoid
  savior.
- Palette: indigo `#172554`, blue `#2563EB`, teal `#0F766E`, amber `#D97706`,
  warm paper `#FFFDF7`, and slate `#334155`.
- SVG text remains live text wherever possible.
- Every visual must still make sense when read through its alt text.
