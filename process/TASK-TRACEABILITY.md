**Scope:** all 54 files in `research/raw/`, the edited paper, evidence atlas,
dashboard, deck, demos, README and process documents

This ledger distinguishes five states that the repository previously collapsed:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Researched** | A report examines the subject and cites sources |
| **Specified** | The public architecture says how the capability belongs in the system |
| **Demonstrated** | A working artifact performs the scoped mechanism |
| **Validated** | A technical benchmark or learner study tests the claim |
| **Productized** | The integrated system works for real learners in its declared setting |

The research and public specification are now aligned. The integrated product is not
built or validated.

## Validated and unvalidated claims

### Validated or externally measured

- Particular purpose-built or human-supervised generative-AI interventions can
  improve proximal outcomes.
- Unguarded assistance can improve practice while degrading later unassisted
  performance in at least one randomized field setting.
- Prior-state variation and the separation between felt and demonstrated learning
  constrain the design.
- Individual mechanism demos render and behave as their pages declare.

### Still hypotheses

- The integrated persistent, multimodal personal faculty improves delayed transfer
  over a strong general model.
- Specialist-agent orchestration outperforms one capable general agent.
- Generated representations can be selected and checked quickly enough for a live
  session.
- The creator/publisher interchange produces a defensible data and distribution loop.
- The system makes polymathic learning materially faster across domains.
- The architecture improves reach and outcomes for the founding SELPA population.
